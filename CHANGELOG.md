# Changelog

## 1.0, November 2026

The first public version.

**The band**
- Four parts (bass, chords, melody, pulse) written from word dials: seven moods, five energies, five feels, five
  shapes, 1,024 variations, any key. Deterministic: the same settings always give the same music.
- Twelve styles, each with its own drum pattern and, where the idiom has one, bass line and chord rhythm: free,
  club, street, funk, island, breeze, fiesta, lounge, roadhouse, motor, rush, drift.
- Eight ways of playing for each part, including arpeggiated chords and a walking bass.

**Playing**
- Take any seat. Four key layouts: EASY (five notes that always fit, the default), SCALE, SNAP and PIANO.
- Time lock: what you play waits for the beat.
- A looping phrase recorder with overdub, undo and snap. Phrases follow the piece if you change its key or mood.

**Performing**
- Mutes and seats on the bar line, solo, fills and builds, five sections, eight scenes, undo and a variation roll.
- A whole new piece from one button.

**Sound**
- 94 instruments on two engines (FM with feedback; an analogue-style voice with two oscillators, a sub-octave,
  drive and a resonant filter), with delayed vibrato, drift, glide and unison.
- Five drums. A chorus and five rooms. A level for each part.

**Keeping and sharing**
- A piece is a 21-character code with a check character; shown large, and typed in on the instrument.
- 64 shipped pieces. Save on demand, with a light that shows when something is unsaved.

**Learning**
- A fourteen-step lesson on the instrument, the training flight.
- Pilot's Notes, a quick reference and an install guide.

**Under it**
- Built on Dinghy (the Felucca and SLOOP platform). Integer arithmetic throughout.
- Checked by about 19 million engine tests, reference pieces compared note for note, a measurement of every
  instrument, the platform's test suite, and a timing run of the heaviest band in an emulator.
